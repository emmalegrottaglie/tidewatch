Tidewatch
Analyzer Planner Tools How it works Source

Plan a week of posts.

List the subreddits you post to. Tidewatch reads each one, finds its best hours, and lays out a week where every post lands at a strong time, with a link to post straight away, a spreadsheet and calendar reminders.

Separate names with commas, spaces or new lines. Up to 25.

Tidewatch is open source under the MIT license. Reddit data comes from the Arctic Shift archive and stays in your browser.
